# TFP-STUDY-3-CONTINGENCY — B3 Steelman Packet: C-SELF-EXPLANATORY 0.1.0

## Identity

- Candidate: `C-SELF-EXPLANATORY`
- Frozen label: **Self-explanatory contingent reality**
- Status: **FROZEN_B3_PACKET__PENDING_EQUAL_STRENGTH_REVIEW**

## Candidate-native / primary formulation

An ultimate contingent item or totality stands in a genuine, non-vicious explanatory relation to itself. It therefore avoids both brute termination and dependence on a distinct necessary foundation. The explanandum remains contingent; the family does not turn it into a necessary truth merely by labeling it self-explanatory.

## Literature status

This exact “contingent ultimate reality explains itself” thesis is not a large named school. The packet therefore treats it as a **synthetic TFP family** anchored in serious literature showing that self-explanation is a live formal/metaphysical notion rather than attributing the full TFP thesis to a single proponent.

## Strongest serious proponent / native sources

1. **Robert Nozick**, *Philosophical Explanations* (Harvard University Press, 1981), especially the discussion of explanatory self-subsumption around p. 115ff.
2. **Yannic Kappes**, “Self-Explanation and Empty-Base Explanation,” *Journal of the American Philosophical Association* 8.3 (2022): 436–453, DOI 10.1017/apa.2021.8. Kappes distinguishes notions of self-explanation and argues that at least some forms can avoid standard impossibility arguments.
3. “Principle of Sufficient Reason,” *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy*, which treats self-explanatory facts as a substantive issue within PSR debates.

No source above is represented as specifically endorsing the full frozen TFP contingent-totality thesis.

## Core truth propositions

1. The stopping item or totality is genuinely contingent.
2. A real item-reflexive explanatory relation obtains.
3. The reflexive relation is not equivalent to lack of explanation.
4. Self-explanation need not entail metaphysical necessity.
5. No distinct necessary ground is required.

## Authority commitments

None. The family is a metaphysical proposal about explanation.

## Internal success conditions

- A coherent account of reflexive explanation must be supplied.
- The explanatory relation must not presuppose temporal self-causation.
- The view must distinguish explanatory self-reference from empty labeling.
- Contingency must remain genuine.
- If the putative self-explanation adds no explanatory content, the family collapses to C-BRUTE.

## Expected evidence / argumentative profile

Support is primarily conceptual: a defensible model of explanation in which reflexivity is possible, plus a worked account showing how an ultimate contingent fact can instantiate it without contradiction or explanatory vacuity.

No empirical confirmation rule is frozen here.

## Strongest recognized objections

1. **Asymmetry / irreflexivity objection:** explanation normally appears to run from explanans to a distinct explanandum or from prior ground to grounded fact.
2. **Presupposition objection:** for a thing to explain itself, it may already have to exist or obtain, making the explanation circular in a vicious way.
3. **Vacuity objection:** “it explains itself” can become a label for refusing explanation rather than supplying one.
4. **Contingency objection:** if the explanation lies wholly in the item, why does that explanation not make the item necessary?
5. **Collapse-to-brute objection:** if reflexive explanation fails, the remaining stopping fact is simply brute.

## Common caricatures explicitly rejected

- Self-explanation is not necessarily self-**causation** in time.
- The family does not say every fact explains itself.
- It does not claim necessity follows from self-explanation.
- It is not the same as C-HOLISTIC: item-reflexivity is essential here.
- It is not merely the assertion that the universe “contains its own causes” in an ordinary causal sense.

## Packet contamination note

The packet deliberately distinguishes the frozen TFP family from narrower Nozick/Kappes models. No source is made to endorse a stronger thesis than it actually supports.
