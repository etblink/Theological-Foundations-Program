CANDIDATE-NATIVE SOURCE STRATA

`C-BRUTE`: sources treating an ultimate contingent fact or totality as requiring no deeper truth-relevant explanation, including the distinction between brutality and present ignorance. Separate stratum for the admitted subvariant in which a law-governed stopping structure is itself contingent and not metaphysically necessary.

`C-REGRESS`: sources defending a one-way nonterminating contingent dependence chain as explanatorily complete, without reclassifying the chain as one brute totality and without a necessary terminus.

`C-HOLISTIC`: mutual, cyclic, structural, coherentist, or non-well-founded but non-item-reflexive dependence. Item-reflexive self-explanation is out of this family and must not be used as its native stratum.

`C-SELF-EXPLANATORY`: sources in which an ultimate contingent item or totality stands in a genuine explanatory relation to itself. Necessary `causa sui` formulations are not native unless contingency of the stopping item is explicit.

`C-GLOBAL-DEFLATION`: local-explanation sufficiency and totality-level existence-explanation deflation while genuine metaphysical contingency remains. This stratum must not be filled by modal anti-realism.

`C-MODAL-DISSOLUTION`: modal anti-realist, fictionalist, framework-dependent, or logical-only positions that deny truth-aptness or applicability of the working question's metaphysical-contingency presupposition without asserting that total reality is necessary.

`C-INDETERMINATE-ULTIMACY`: ontic or worldly indeterminacy applied to ultimacy itself. Epistemic underdetermination and Protocol B6 outcome language are not native evidence.

`C-NECESSITARIAN`: sources asserting metaphysical necessity of the relevant totality, with apparent contingency treated as internal, perspectival, derivative, or epistemic.

`C-INDEXICAL-PLURALITY`: indexical contingency inside a plurality of loci or possibilities that is the stopping structure and has no external selector. Sources that add a selector belong elsewhere.

`C-NEC-IMPERSONAL-CONCRETE`: necessary concrete, non-agentive substances, facts, processes, matter, or spacetime-like foundations. Cardinality variants — single, plural, layered — and relation variants — causal, grounding, constitutive, or another specified relation — each need native access.

`C-NEC-AGENTIVE`: necessary intentional or mind-like agency used only as an explanatory relation to contingent reality. Native strata must be available without perfect goodness, omniscience, omnipotence, providence, worship-worthiness, or identity with any religion's God. Non-Abrahamic and non-classical-theist agentive formulations are required so the family is not sourced only as theism.

`C-NEC-IMPERSONAL-PRINCIPLE`: separate native strata for abstract or mathematical structure, law-like or metaphysical principle, and modal-space or modal-constraint variants, each showing explanatory or grounding force rather than mere description.

`C-AXIARCHIC`: non-agentive value, goodness, optimality, or evaluative requiredness as itself explanatory. Personal perfect-goodness arguments are not native.

ADVERSE / CRITICAL SOURCE STRATA

`C-BRUTE`: PSR and explanatory-realist objections, arguments that brutality is a defect rather than ignorance, and objections aimed at contingent nomological stopping.

`C-REGRESS`: vicious-regress and well-foundedness objections, plus arguments that a nonterminating chain aggregates into a brute totality.

`C-HOLISTIC`: asymmetry and noncircularity objections, collapse-into-brute objections, and arguments that mutual dependence is not explanation.

`C-SELF-EXPLANATORY`: objections that reflexive explanation is not genuine explanation, and contingency-preservation objections that force collapse into `C-BRUTE`.

`C-GLOBAL-DEFLATION`: totality-fact and global-PSR objections, including arguments that memberwise explanation leaves a remainder.

`C-MODAL-DISSOLUTION`: defenses of truth-apt de re metaphysical contingency, and objections that the view covertly becomes necessitarianism or changes the question.

`C-INDETERMINATE-ULTIMACY`: determinacy-first objections, semantic-only accounts of apparent indeterminacy, and objections that the claim is only epistemic underdetermination.

`C-NECESSITARIAN`: defenses of genuine metaphysical contingency and objections to treating all apparent contingency as ignorance or perspective.

`C-INDEXICAL-PLURALITY`: actuality-privilege and external-selector objections, plus objections that the plurality is itself a contingent or brute remainder.

`C-NEC-IMPERSONAL-CONCRETE`: category and modal-collapse objections, why-this-concrete-item objections, and challenges from principle-only and agentive alternatives without treating those alternatives as established.

`C-NEC-AGENTIVE`: modal-collapse objections, intentionality-inadmissibility objections, no-bridge objections from necessity to agency, and property-smuggling objections against importing goodness, knowledge, power, providence, or religious identity.

`C-NEC-IMPERSONAL-PRINCIPLE`: instantiation-gap, causal-inertness, and descriptive-not-explanatory objections for each subvariant.

`C-AXIARCHIC`: value-to-being gap objections, evaluative anti-realism, agentive-reduction objections, and subsumption objections toward impersonal principle.

BACKGROUND SOURCE STRATA

`BG-EXPLANATORY-REALISM`: robust metaphysical-explanation and grounding sources, pluralist anti-monist sources, and deflationary or pragmatic explanation sources.

`BG-PSR-SCOPE`: global, restricted, local-only, and nondefault PSR sources, including classical and contemporary formulations and Humean limit objections.

`BG-TOTALITY-EXPLANANDUM`: distinct-totality, local-sufficiency, and no-determinate-totality sources, including aggregation and Hume-Edwards disputes. Semantic non-truth-aptness stays with `C-MODAL-DISSOLUTION`, not this dimension.

`BG-DEPENDENCE-STRUCTURE`: well-founded, infinite-regress-complete, holistic nonreflexive, and reflexive-permissible sources. Relation-specific viciousness must be representable. No global viciousness rule is a source-selection premise.

`BG-MODAL-STATUS`: de re metaphysical, logical or conceptual only, nomic or physical only, epistemic-only, and deflationary modal sources. The deflationary state is a gate variant and should not be crossed with every lower-order existence combination.

`BG-ACTUALITY-PRIVILEGE`: privileged-actuality and indexical or non-privileged actuality sources. This is not the native stratum for `C-INDEXICAL-PLURALITY`.

`BG-ABSTRACT-PRINCIPLE-FORCE`: explanatory, constraint-only, and descriptive accounts of abstracta, laws, structures, and modal principles.

`BG-GROUND-CATEGORY`: concrete-required, abstract-eligible, and category-open sources.

`BG-EXPLANATION-RELATION`: separate support and challenge classes for causal or sustaining, grounding, and nomic relations; for contrastive versus noncontrastive sufficiency; and for intentional admissibility versus inadmissibility.

`BG-ONTOLOGY-ADMISSIBILITY`: concrete causal-closure, methodological-preference-only, and open-admissibility sources. Naturalism and supernaturalism labels are not source classes.

`BG-TEMPORAL-ORIGIN-LINK`: sources distinguishing modal contingency from temporal beginning, and sources that would independently license a beginning premise. Physics-specific cosmologies are not default inputs.

`BG-MODAL-LOGIC`: S5, weaker T or S4, and unfixed-logic sources, used only for inferences that move from possibility to necessity.

`BG-AGENTIVE-MODAL-LINK`: contingent-output, necessitating-output, and agency-nonfundamental sources, without imported divine perfections.

`BG-AXIOLOGICAL-FORCE`: ontological value-to-existence, action-only reasons, normative-only value, and anti-realist sources.

`BG-METAPHYSICAL-INDETERMINACY`: ontic-indeterminacy-possible, determinate-even-if-unknown, and semantic-only sources.
