## Candidate-native / primary formulation

A metaphysically necessary non-agentive principle, structure, law, modal space, mathematical structure, or comparable non-concrete ground explains relevant contingent reality. The explanans is not a choosing mind and is not merely a description of the contingent facts; it must possess genuine explanatory or grounding force.

The frozen family retains three provisional subvariants: abstract/mathematical/structural; law-like/metaphysical-principle; and modal-space/constraint.

## Literature status

This is a **synthetic umbrella family** joining several serious literatures that share a non-agentive structural/principled explanatory role. No cited author is represented as endorsing every subvariant or the exact Study-3 family.

## Strongest serious proponent / native sources

1. **Max Tegmark**, “The Mathematical Universe” (2007/2008), advancing the thesis that physical reality is an abstract mathematical structure. This anchors the mathematical/structural subvariant, not by itself the full TFP grounding thesis.
2. **James Ladyman, Don Ross, David Spurrett, and John Collier**, *Every Thing Must Go: Metaphysics Naturalized* (Oxford University Press, 2007), defending ontic structural realism in which objective modal/relational structure is fundamental.
3. **Martin Grajner**, “Grounding, Metaphysical Laws, and Structure,” *Analytic Philosophy* 62.4 (2021): 376–395, for a serious account connecting grounding to metaphysical laws.
4. *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy*, “Metaphysical Grounding,” for views on which grounding facts are mediated by essence, explanation, or necessary metaphysical laws.

## Core truth propositions

1. A relevant necessary non-agentive principle/structure exists or obtains.
2. It is not merely a convenient representation of contingent reality.
3. It has genuine explanatory, grounding, constitutive, or constraining force.
4. Intentional agency is not required for the ultimate explanatory role.
5. Its necessity is metaphysical, not merely the consequence of contingent physical laws.

## Authority commitments

None. Mathematical, structural, or law-like formalisms count only insofar as the metaphysical thesis is defended, not by scientific prestige alone.

## Internal success conditions

- The candidate must say what kind of entity/fact the principle or structure is.
- The alleged necessity must be warranted.
- The principle must explain rather than merely redescribe.
- The relation between abstract/non-concrete structure and concrete contingent reality must be intelligible.
- Distinct subvariants must remain in one family only while they share the same necessary truth-bearing commitments; a later genuine divergence requires C4-controlled splitting.

## Expected evidence / argumentative profile

Support would primarily be theoretical and structural: an independently motivated ontology in which a necessary structure, law, or constraint is fundamental and has explanatory force with respect to contingent reality. Empirical success of a mathematical theory alone is not enough to establish the metaphysical ground.

## Strongest recognized objections

1. **Abstract-causation/grounding objection:** abstract structures may describe possibilities without producing or grounding concrete actuality.
2. **Instantiation objection:** even if a mathematical structure necessarily exists, why is it physically instantiated or concrete?
3. **Descriptive/explanatory objection:** laws and structures may summarize patterns rather than explain why the patterns obtain.
4. **Modal-status objection:** the alleged principle may itself be contingent or framework-relative.
5. **Subvariant heterogeneity objection:** mathematical structure, metaphysical law, and modal constraint may eventually prove to have different necessary commitments and require splitting.
6. **Agentive-selection objection:** if multiple outcomes satisfy the same necessary principle, the principle may underdetermine which contingent reality obtains.

## Common caricatures explicitly rejected

- The family is not “physics explains everything.”
- It is not automatically the Mathematical Universe Hypothesis.
- It does not equate equations with concrete objects.
- It does not posit a hidden agent.
- It does not assume all laws of nature are metaphysically necessary.
- It does not collapse value-based requiredness into this family when value is explanatorily essential; that belongs to C-AXIARCHIC.
