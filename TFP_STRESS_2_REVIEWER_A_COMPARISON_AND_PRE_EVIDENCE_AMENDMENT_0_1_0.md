## 4. Accepted pre-evidence amendments

### A1 — Separate the event study from specific divine-agent attribution

**Accepted.**

TFP-STRESS-2 0.1.1 will authorize, if G0 is later approved, only the Q1 event comparison.

The former Q2 question — whether God specifically raised Jesus — is retained as a **future conditional study question**, not as an active comparison in the same G0 authorization.

Reason:
- Q2 requires its own candidate/causal-class universe, background register, necessary-proposition map, and discriminators;
- keeping Q2 inside the same live comparison risks asymmetric gating and importing excluded life/authority claims after seeing Q1 evidence;
- a positive or strong Q1 result would be the proper trigger for a separately preregistered Q2 study.

This is a narrowing pre-evidence amendment, not a conclusion.

### A2 — Split veridical non-embodied encounter from subjective experiential origin

**Accepted.**

The original `C-V` conflated two materially different propositions:
- a real extramental postmortem referent encountered in a non-embodied mode;
- a sincere subjective experience without such a referent.

The final candidate universe will therefore contain:

- `R`: embodied post-death living state;
- `V`: veridical non-embodied postmortem encounter;
- `H`: subjective experiential/psychological origin.

This prevents both R and H from absorbing the rival ontology they are meant to test.

### A3 — Preserve R submodels

**Accepted.**

`R` is frozen at the weakest shared proposition level required by Q1.

At least two separately burdened submodels are preserved:
- `R-PHYS`: physical-continuity form for which a body/tomb consequence is necessary as specified in its packet;
- `R-TRANS`: transformed/pneumatic embodied form that does not automatically require the same tomb proposition.

A vulnerable submodel-specific proposition may not be dropped merely because the umbrella R family can be stated more weakly.

### A4 — Sharpen L against H

**Accepted.**

`L` will mean an interpretive/scriptural/narrative-development origin in which claimed encounter traditions are secondary or non-primary to the origin of the proclamation.

`H` requires sincere subjective experiences to be materially causal at origin.

The final necessary-proposition map must prevent either candidate from borrowing the other's causal sequence after evidence exposure.

### A5 — Expand and separately burden the non-death family

**Accepted with a control.**

The study will preserve:
- `S1`: natural survival/rescue after crucifixion;
- `S2`: substitution or misidentified victim;
- `S3`: divine rescue/non-death model.

These are tracked as **separately status-bearing submodels**, not a flexible family allowed to swap necessary propositions.

If a submodel lacks sufficient specification or serious traceable formulation at final candidate-packet review, it may be excluded only by a frozen, independently reviewed B4 record.

### A6 — Admit one bounded heterogeneous composite

**Accepted with stronger anti-tailoring controls.**

The original design barred a free-form composite. Reviewer A correctly notes that a genuinely multicausal origin is itself a serious structural possibility.

The final candidate set will therefore admit `C-HET` only under a frozen module map.

Initial module architecture:

- `M-EXP-IND`: subjective individual experience module;
- `M-EXP-SOC`: social/group experience-report formation module;
- `M-INT`: scriptural/theological interpretation module;
- `M-NARR`: later narrative/memory development module;
- `M-BODY`: burial/body-disposition uncertainty or ordinary body-disposition module.

Before G0 authorization the final `C-HET` packet must map each necessary proposition/evidence class to exactly one primary module, identify any explicitly permitted interaction, and freeze:
- module definitions;
- module-to-proposition map;
- independent warrant required for each mechanism;
- interaction rules;
- prohibition on adding, deleting, or reassigning modules after evidence exposure except through C4.

Deliberate fabrication is **not** an open optional module inside C-HET; it remains the separate F candidate unless specifically preregistered before G0.

The original `C-BX` body-disposition-plus-nonbodily model is therefore demoted from a stand-alone full candidate and absorbed as a constrained possible configuration inside C-HET or L where the final packet supports that placement.

### A7 — Mythicism/non-historicity becomes a node-level rival, not a full candidate

**Accepted.**

Total non-historicity remains outside the full Q1 candidate comparison because it changes the target question.

However, it must not be silently assumed away.

The final coverage map will include a direct historical route for:
- historical existence of the subject;
- subject identity;
- crucifixion attribution.

A frozen B4 exclusion record must state why non-historicity is treated as a node-level rival rather than a full event candidate.

No "historical Jesus existed" proposition is treated as established merely for tractability.

### A8 — Split crucifixion from death and remove the overbroad shared-floor formulation

**Accepted.**

The original draft's `SF1` combined historical subject and crucifixion too broadly.

The final map will distinguish:
- `P-HIST-JESUS`: historical subject exists;
- `P-CRUC`: the subject underwent Roman crucifixion;
- `P-DEATH`: the subject genuinely died from/around that event.

Because S2/S3 can deny or relocate P-CRUC/P-DEATH, those propositions are not universal shared floor across every final submodel.

### A9 — Operationalize the Q1 causal threshold

**Accepted.**

Define:

- `FOUNDING_PROCLAMATION_STRATUM`: the earliest recoverable resurrection/exaltation proclamation attributable to the founding Jesus-following movement, without assuming in advance whether its locus was Jerusalem, Judea, Galilee, or another network;
- `FOUNDING_ENCOUNTER_STREAM`: a claimed encounter/experience stream alleged to precede or materially contribute to that founding proclamation.

For Q1 / `R_EVENT` to be true within scope, the study must support all of:

1. `P-DEATH`: Jesus genuinely died;
2. `P-R-BODY`: Jesus subsequently existed in an embodied living postmortem state;
3. `P-R-FOUNDING-ENCOUNTER`: at least one **pre-Pauline founding encounter stream** materially implicated in the origin of the founding proclamation was an encounter with that embodied living Jesus;
4. `P-R-CAUSAL`: that embodied encounter stream materially caused or generated the founding resurrection proclamation.

Paul's later first-person experience may be evidentially important but **cannot by itself satisfy item 3 or 4**, because the proclamation predates Paul's participation by the study's own target structure.

Later group or narrative claims are not silently made necessary unless a candidate submodel explicitly requires them.

### A10 — Define neutral study vocabulary

**Accepted.**

Outcome-neutral terms are preferred in study prose:
- "claimed encounter";
- "experience report";
- "proclamation claim";
- "body/tomb claim".

Use "appearance", "hallucination", "resurrection", or equivalent loaded terms only:
- when naming a candidate's own claim;
- when quoting/paraphrasing a source;
- where the operational definition is explicit.

No term may smuggle veridicality, pathology, or embodiment into a proposition.

### A11 — Define "earliest" operationally

**Accepted.**

"Founding/earliest proclamation" means the earliest recoverable proclamation stratum attributable to the founding movement under the frozen source-critical rules.

"Founding/earliest experience report" means the earliest recoverable claimed-encounter/experience stream argued to be causally upstream of or contemporaneous with that proclamation.

The study will not presuppose a particular geographic locus or named witness set merely by definition.

### A12 — Expand the live background/dimension register

**Accepted in principle; final inclusion remains subject to Reviewer B/C control.**

The revised design must distinguish at least:

1. ontology / causal closure vs metaphysical openness;
2. anomalous-testimony prior/likelihood framework;
3. historiographical method;
4. source dating/dependence framework;
5. experiential-explanation capacity;
6. crucifixion/burial-practice model;
7. Second Temple resurrection/exaltation concept model;
8. Pauline-body interpretation where outcome-material;
9. epistemology of testimony;
10. reference-class/base-rate assumptions.

Q2-only agent-identification backgrounds are deferred with Q2.

Any empirical uncertainty masquerading as a "background" must instead be treated as an ordinary evidence proposition. Reviewer C must review granularity and interactions.

### A13 — Expand claim typing

**Accepted.**

Add explicitly:
- linguistic typing for outcome-material formula/substrate claims;
- interpretive/textual typing for what a source says about embodiment;
- metaphysical typing for whether embodied postmortem existence occurred;
- revelation trigger where a candidate treats a claimed revelation as authority;
- prophecy trigger only where a candidate actually makes a predictive-fulfillment claim;
- authority/canon only where authority is invoked as a premise.

"Scholarly consensus", "minimal facts", and guild authority are not evidence types.

### A14 — Preserve tractability-sensitive claims as unsettled

**Accepted.**

The G0 packet may not presuppose as established:
- empty tomb;
- Joseph of Arimathea burial;
- a group of five hundred as a historical event;
- James as an initial skeptic;
- Paul's persecutor-to-convert sequence;
- martyrdom claims as evidence of truth or sincerity;
- a precise within-N-years date for any formula;
- a universal Jewish exclusion of individual resurrection;
- crucifixion death certainty;
- any preselected "minimal facts" list.

Such propositions receive ordinary source/sufficiency treatment if outcome-material.

### A15 — Keep the insufficiency threshold already frozen

**Accepted with no change.**

Reviewer A's cautions reinforce the existing 0.1.0 rule:
- weak/neutral but adequately covered evidence is not `INSUFFICIENT_SIGNAL`;
- true access/provenance/coverage failure that prevents valid application of a required rule is.

This remains a mandatory Reviewer B review surface.
