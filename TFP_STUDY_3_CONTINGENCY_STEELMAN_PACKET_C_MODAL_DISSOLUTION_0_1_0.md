# TFP-STUDY-3-CONTINGENCY — B3 Steelman Packet: C-MODAL-DISSOLUTION 0.1.0

## Identity

- Candidate: `C-MODAL-DISSOLUTION`
- Frozen label: **Modal contingency-presupposition dissolution**
- Status: **FROZEN_B3_PACKET__PENDING_EQUAL_STRENGTH_REVIEW**

## Candidate-native / primary formulation

A central metaphysical-contingency presupposition of the working question is not truth-apt or not applicable under the candidate's admissible modal framework. The position does not thereby assert that total reality is metaphysically necessary. It challenges the question's use of metaphysical contingency at a prior level.

## Literature status

This is a **synthetic TFP family** anchored in serious anti-realist and deflationary literature about metaphysical modality. No single cited author is treated as endorsing the full Study-3 formulation.

## Strongest serious proponent / native sources

1. **Charles S. Chihara**, *The Worlds of Possibility: Modal Realism and the Semantics of Modal Logic* (Oxford University Press, 1998/2001), especially the treatment of anti-modal realism.
2. **Simon Blackburn**, *Essays in Quasi-Realism* (Oxford University Press, 1993), as part of the literature on anti-realist treatment of modal discourse.
3. *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy*, “The Epistemology of Modality,” which distinguishes realism from anti-realism and notes views on which metaphysical-modal statements are not straightforwardly truth-evaluable.

Modal fictionalism about possible worlds alone is not enough for this family, because many modal fictionalists remain realists about ordinary modal truth.

## Core truth propositions

1. The Study-3 question relies on at least one metaphysical-contingency presupposition.
2. That presupposition is not truth-apt or is inapplicable under the correct modal framework.
3. This is a substantive claim about modal discourse/reality, not investigator uncertainty.
4. The family need not claim that the totality is necessary.
5. Merely rejecting a global explanatory demand while retaining ordinary contingency belongs instead to C-GLOBAL-DEFLATION.

## Authority commitments

None. The family depends on a theory of metaphysical modality and modal language.

## Internal success conditions

- Identify the exact contingency presupposition being dissolved.
- Supply a coherent account of modal discourse once that presupposition is rejected.
- Avoid sliding from “we cannot know whether P is possible” to “P is not truth-apt/applicable.”
- Avoid collapsing into necessitarianism.
- Preserve enough non-modal structure for the Study-3 claim itself to remain substantive rather than merely verbal.

## Expected evidence / argumentative profile

The principal support would be semantic/metaphysical: an independently motivated account of modality on which the relevant contingency claim is not literally apt for the role the working question assigns it. The family does not predict a distinctive empirical observation merely by being modal-deflationary.

## Strongest recognized objections

1. **Ordinary-modal-practice objection:** counterfactual and modal reasoning appears indispensable and often truth-directed.
2. **Self-undermining objection:** the anti-realist may rely on modal notions to state or defend anti-realism.
3. **Scope objection:** anti-realism about possible worlds does not automatically entail anti-realism about metaphysical possibility or contingency.
4. **Replacement objection:** dissolving one modal framework requires explaining why an alternative semantics or metaphysics is preferable.
5. **Question-evasion objection:** the view can look like refusing the contingency question rather than answering it unless the modal diagnosis is independently warranted.

## Common caricatures explicitly rejected

- This is not “we do not know whether reality is contingent.”
- It is not the B6 outcome UNDERDETERMINED.
- It does not automatically assert that everything is necessary.
- It is not merely skepticism about possible-world entities.
- It is not identical to global-explanation deflation.

## Packet contamination note

The packet deliberately uses anti-modal-realist literature as an anchor without attributing TFP's broader dissolution family to any one author.
