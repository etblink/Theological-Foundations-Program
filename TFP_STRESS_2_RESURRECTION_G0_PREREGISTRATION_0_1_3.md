# A14 — Stop / underdetermination / insufficient signal / reopen

## INSUFFICIENT_SIGNAL

Use `INSUFFICIENT_SIGNAL` only when a prospectively designated access/provenance/coverage failure prevents a required sufficiency rule, candidate-status rule, direction rule, or mandatory background rerun from being validly applied after documented effort.

Do not use ISS merely because:
- evidence is weak;
- a claim is not established;
- evidence is adequately covered but neutral;
- rivals remain live;
- a complete search fails to favor a candidate.

For TFP-STRESS-2:

- "frozen search completed without sufficient signal" counts as UNMAKEABLE only when the shortfall is due to inaccessibility, true provenance failure, or missing/non-extant material in a prospectively designated truth-critical stratum.
- accessible, provenance-adequate weak/neutral evidence is `NEUTRAL_OR_NONDISCRIMINATING` or receives the applicable proposition disposition.
- bounded date/authorship/dependence uncertainty is propagated through sensitivity/confidence and is not provenance failure.
- unperformed search is `COVERAGE_INCOMPLETE` and cannot close as ISS.
- a CRITICAL discriminator not preregistered INFEASIBLE cannot later produce ISS merely because the result is inconvenient; the comparison-effort record and source-plan truth-critical rules govern.

## UNDERDETERMINATION

Use `UNDERDETERMINED` when coverage is adequate but:
- multiple live candidates remain without robust ordering;
- a genuine ranking flip occurs across admitted live backgrounds;
- mixed tradeoffs remain unresolved;
- evidence is adequately covered but nondiscriminating.

## Stop

Stop when:
- truth-critical strata are complete/materially complete;
- necessary propositions have dispositions;
- outcome-relevant discriminators have reviewed results or bounded UNMAKEABLE records;
- mandatory background reruns are complete;
- remaining accessible material is mainly dependent/repetitive or not expected to change a truth-critical result.

## Reopen

Reopen only for:
- materially new primary evidence;
- materially changed provenance/dating/authorship/dependence;
- newly accessible truth-critical source;
- credible challenge to a truth-critical sufficiency/direction decision;
- disconfirmation/replication of an empirical mechanism claim;
- later TFP work that independently triggers a bounded reconsideration.
