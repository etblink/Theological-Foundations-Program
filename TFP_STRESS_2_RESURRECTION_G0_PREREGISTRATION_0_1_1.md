# A1 — Exact truth question

TFP-STRESS-2 0.1.1 contains **one active truth comparison**.

## Q1 — Event proposition

> **Does the best publicly adjudicable historical evidence warrant the proposition that Jesus of Nazareth genuinely died and subsequently existed again in an embodied living postmortem state, such that at least one pre-Pauline founding claimed-encounter stream involving that embodied living Jesus materially caused or generated the founding resurrection proclamation?**

Define:

`R_EVENT` = the proposition above.

Operational definitions:

- **FOUNDING_PROCLAMATION_STRATUM:** the earliest recoverable resurrection/exaltation proclamation attributable to the founding Jesus-following movement under the frozen source-critical rules, without assuming in advance that its locus was Jerusalem, Judea, Galilee, or another network.
- **FOUNDING_ENCOUNTER_STREAM:** a claimed encounter/experience stream alleged to precede or materially contribute to that founding proclamation.
- **embodied living postmortem state:** a state stronger than dream, bereavement vision, purely internal religious experience, literary symbol, memory construction, non-veridical apparition, or survival without death.

For `R_EVENT` to be true within scope, all of the following must be supported at the required route threshold:

1. `P-DEATH`: Jesus genuinely died;
2. `P-R-BODY`: Jesus subsequently existed in an embodied living postmortem state;
3. `P-R-FOUNDING-ENCOUNTER`: at least one pre-Pauline founding encounter stream materially implicated in the origin of the founding proclamation was an encounter with that embodied living Jesus;
4. `P-R-CAUSAL`: that encounter stream materially caused or generated the founding resurrection proclamation.

Paul's later first-person experience may be evidentially relevant but **cannot by itself satisfy items 3 or 4**.

Later group or narrative claims are not silently made necessary unless a frozen candidate submodel explicitly requires them.

Neutral terminology is preferred:
- "claimed encounter";
- "experience report";
- "proclamation claim";
- "body/tomb claim".

Terms such as "appearance", "hallucination", or "resurrection" may be used for a candidate's own formulation, a source's wording, or where an operational definition is explicit. No term may smuggle veridicality, pathology, or embodiment into the result.

## Deferred future question — specific causal/agent attribution

The former Q2 question:

# A2 — Scope

## Time period

Primary event window:
- the crucifixion period conventionally placed in the early first century CE;
- the emergence of the earliest resurrection proclamation and appearance traditions.

Primary source-evaluation window:
- first-century sources and source layers;
- early second-century material through approximately 150 CE where it can bear on transmission, dependence, reception, or development.

Later material may be used for:
- textual transmission;
- reception history;
- source dependence;
- preservation arguments;
- explicit later interpretation.

Later material may not be silently projected backward.

## Geography

Primary:
- Roman Judea and Galilee;
- Jerusalem;
- early eastern Mediterranean Christian networks materially relevant to the earliest surviving sources.

Other geography enters only when it bears on transmission, provenance, or source dependence.

## Languages

As relevant:
- Koine Greek;
- Aramaic/Hebrew substrate claims;
- Latin for Roman evidence.

The study does not require the program lead personally to possess specialist fluency. Specialist scholarship, critical editions, translations, and linguistic review must be provenance-recorded.

## Source universe

The frozen source plan must include, as relevant:
- earliest Christian textual sources;
- embedded earlier traditions/creedal or formulaic material where critically defensible;
- gospel/Acts traditions with explicit source-dependence analysis;
- early noncanonical Christian evidence inside the scope window;
- near-contemporary non-Christian evidence relevant to death, movement existence, or context;
- Roman crucifixion/execution evidence;
- Jewish burial/funerary evidence relevant to the case;
- textual-critical evidence for outcome-material passages;
- historical scholarship across confessional and nonconfessional positions;
- psychology/cognitive science relevant to bereavement experiences, visions, memory, group reports, contagion, and testimony;
- philosophy of testimony, historical inference, miracle/anomaly reasoning, and causal explanation;
- candidate-native/proponent scholarship;
- serious adverse/critical scholarship;
- methodological scholarship on ancient-history inference.

No source is elevated because it is canonical, extracanonical, skeptical, confessional, traditional, popular, or institutionally prestigious.

## Explicit exclusions

This study does **not** directly adjudicate:
- whether Christianity as a whole is true;
- the Trinity;
- incarnation as a complete doctrine;
- atonement theories;
- salvation/soteriology;
- biblical inspiration or inerrancy as global doctrines;
- papal, conciliar, denominational, or ecclesial authority;
- prophecy fulfillment unrelated to the specific event question;

- whether every gospel detail is historically true;

# A14 — Stop, underdetermination, insufficient signal, reopen

## Frozen insufficiency threshold — designed to implement qualified-audit MINOR-1 constraint

Use `INSUFFICIENT_SIGNAL` only when **access, provenance, or coverage failure prevents the study from validly applying a required sufficiency rule, candidate-status rule, DIRECTION_RULE, or required background rerun at all** after documented effort.

Examples:
- an outcome-material source stratum required by the frozen plan cannot be accessed and no adequate substitute exists;
- provenance failure makes a CRITICAL evidence class uninterpretable;
- a CRITICAL comparison cannot reach MAKEABLE and remains outcome-relevant;
- background-sensitive inputs required for a mandatory rerun cannot be evaluated.

Do **not** use `INSUFFICIENT_SIGNAL` merely because:
- accessible evidence is weak;
- a claim is not established;
- adequately covered evidence is neutral;
- rivals remain live;
- historical evidence cannot distinguish between adequately evaluated candidates.

Those cases route to proposition dispositions such as `NOT_ESTABLISHED` or `UNDERDETERMINED`, and then to the N3 decision table.

This threshold must be independently reviewed before final G0 freeze.

## Underdetermination condition

Use `UNDERDETERMINED` when:
- required coverage is adequate;
- relevant candidates remain structurally/evidentially live;
- frozen discriminators fail to establish robust dominance;
- framework dependence produces a genuine ranking flip;
- mixed tradeoffs remain unresolved.

## Stop condition

Stop rather than expanding scope when:
- all frozen source strata are COMPLETE or MATERIALLY_COMPLETE_WITH_LISTED_GAPS;
- all necessary propositions have dispositions;
- all outcome-relevant discriminators have valid reviewed results or documented UNMAKEABLE status;
- all mandatory background reruns are complete;
- further accessible evidence is predominantly dependent/repetitive or not expected to change a truth-critical disposition.

## Reopen if

Reopen a closed/held result only for:
- materially new primary evidence;
- materially changed dating/authorship/source-dependence result;
- newly accessible outcome-material source;
- credible challenge to a truth-critical sufficiency/direction decision;
- successful replication or disconfirmation of an empirical/psychological claim material to the outcome;
- a later TFP study requiring bounded reconsideration.
