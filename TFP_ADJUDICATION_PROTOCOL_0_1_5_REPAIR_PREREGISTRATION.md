## 2. MAJOR repair scope

### M1 — discriminator completeness and inferential bridge

Repair by:

- requiring every necessary truth-bearing proposition of every admitted candidate to be covered before G0 closes;
- allowing either at least one preregistered comparative discriminator OR a documented non-comparative evidential route where comparison is not meaningful;
- requiring independent certification of that coverage map;
- tying `BEST_SUPPORTED` and dominance to the complete necessary-proposition/discriminator map;
- preventing a candidate from winning because only its favorable necessary propositions were selected for CRITICAL comparison.

## 3. Same-surface MINOR repair scope

### D1 — exclusive discriminator tiers
Define a single mutually exclusive priority test:
- CRITICAL if the discriminator can determine candidate adequacy or the truth status of a necessary proposition;
- MATERIAL only if it changes relative warrant without determining adequacy or truth status of a necessary proposition;
- CONTEXTUAL otherwise.

### D2 — unified adequacy consequences
Align A12, Phase F, N2 and N3 so that:
- contradiction of a necessary proposition makes the candidate inadequate;
- an undefeated TRUTH_CRITICAL_DEFEATER makes the candidate ineligible for `BEST_SUPPORTED`, `CLOSEST_TO_TRUTH`, and `TRUTH_WARRANTED`;
- EVIDENCE_AGAINST on a necessary proposition is classified consistently.

### D3 — MAKEABLE versus NEUTRAL
Reserve:
- `UNMAKEABLE` for inadequate coverage/access;
- `NEUTRAL_OR_NONDISCRIMINATING` for adequate coverage with no directional result.

### D4 — exactly one adequate candidate
Define a valid outcome when exactly one admitted candidate remains adequate.

### D5 — background flip information preservation
If a candidate remains BEST_SUPPORTED under every live background but truth-warrant eligibility changes across backgrounds:
- preserve the robust BEST_SUPPORTED ranking;
- separately mark truth-warrant as `FRAMEWORK_DEPENDENT`;
- do not collapse the entire study to undifferentiated underdetermination.

### D6 — CRITICAL feasibility
Require a pre-G0 feasibility check for every CRITICAL discriminator.
A discriminator known to be unobservable/unrecoverable cannot be CRITICAL in the confirmatory study unless `INSUFFICIENT_SIGNAL` is itself the preregistered intended consequence.
