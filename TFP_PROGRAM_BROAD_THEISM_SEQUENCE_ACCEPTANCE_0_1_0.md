## 1. Human-owner direction

The human owner approved the Program Lead's recommended strategic direction after discussion of the gravity and portfolio effects of the next study.

Accepted high-level sequence:

1. preserve TFP-STRESS-2 as canonically CLOSED;
2. next investigate a major **affirmative** argument concerning ultimate reality / necessary foundation;
3. then investigate a major **adverse** challenge to personal theism through divine hiddenness;
4. keep the two studies separately preregistered and separately adjudicated;
5. only after multiple methodologically distinct canonical results exist should TFP consider a bounded cross-study synthesis;
6. do not treat study order as evidential weighting, quota-balancing, or a presumption that one side must win.

## 2. Governing rationale

TFP exists to discover what is true, not to defend Christianity, naturalism, supernaturalism, skepticism, or any other prior framework.

The program has completed one canonical bounded adjudication in a historical/testimonial/miracle-adjacent evidentiary ecology. The next study should therefore add genuinely different information.

A deliberate affirmative/adverse pair around broader theism helps avoid an early portfolio in which:
- Christianity-specific historical questions dominate;
- only adverse challenges to theism are investigated;
- only affirmative natural-theology arguments are investigated;
- later Christian studies silently inherit an untested background ontology.

This sequencing is a program-design safeguard, not an evidential balancing rule. Each study may independently support, weaken, fail to establish, or leave underdetermined any relevant claim.

## 3. Provisional Study 3 — Contingency / necessary foundation

Working ID:

`TFP-STUDY-3-CONTINGENCY`

Provisional bounded question:

> **Does the existence of contingent reality warrant positing a necessary explanatory ground or foundation rather than accepting contingent reality as brute, and if so, what properties of that foundation are independently warranted without presupposing theism?**

Key intended discipline:

- do not equate "necessary foundation" with "God" by definition;
- distinguish explanatory necessity from logical, metaphysical, nomological, and causal necessity;
- treat brute-contingency views, necessary-cosmos views, abstract/structural grounds, law-like foundations, impersonal grounds, and personal/agentive grounds as potentially live until G0 candidate-completeness work says otherwise;
- require every attributed property—unity, agency, mentality, goodness, power, knowledge, personality, worship-worthiness, etc.—to be separately warranted;
- permit the result "necessary foundation warranted, classical/personal theism not warranted";
- permit the result "no necessary foundation warranted";
- permit genuine underdetermination.

This wording is not yet G0-frozen.

## 4. Provisional Study 4 — Divine hiddenness / perfect love

Working ID:

`TFP-STUDY-4-HIDDENNESS`

This study remains strategically favored after Study 3 because it supplies a methodologically different adverse test of personal theism.

It is not yet G0-authorized.
