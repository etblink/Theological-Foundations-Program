## 2. Recommended next study

Working ID:

`TFP-STUDY-3-CONTINGENCY`

Provisional bounded question:

> **Does the existence of contingent reality warrant positing a necessary explanatory ground or foundation rather than accepting contingent reality as brute, and if so, what properties of that foundation are independently warranted without presupposing theism?**

## 3. Why this study is next

TFP-STRESS-2 exercised historical, textual, testimonial, causal, psychological, and miracle-adjacent reasoning.

A contingency/foundation study would exercise a substantially different domain:

- metaphysical explanation;
- modal reasoning;
- explanatory principles;
- necessary vs contingent being/reality;
- brute-fact positions;
- personal vs impersonal foundational hypotheses.

It can therefore test whether TFP's method transfers to a non-historical domain while adding positive-evidence information relevant to broad theism.

## 4. Hard discipline for G0 design

If G0 is later authorized, the architecture should prevent the common slide:

`NECESSARY FOUNDATION -> GOD -> CLASSICAL THEISM`

Each inferential step must be separately represented.

At minimum, G0 should keep open candidate families such as:

- brute contingent reality;
- necessary cosmos/reality;
- necessary abstract or structural ground;
- necessary law-like ground;
- necessary impersonal concrete ground;
- necessary personal/agentive ground;
- plural or layered foundations;
- other candidates discovered by blind reviewer elicitation.

The list above is a design seed only, not a frozen candidate universe.

## 5. Properties must be separately earned

If any necessary foundation is supported, the study must separately ask what follows regarding:

- uniqueness;
- unity;
- causal efficacy;
- temporality/atemporality;
- mentality;
- intentionality;
- agency;
- knowledge;
- power;
- goodness;
- personhood;
- providence;
- worship-worthiness.

No property may be imported merely because a candidate is called "God."

## 6. Legitimate outcomes

The study must permit, among others:

- no necessary foundation warranted;
- some necessary foundation warranted but its nature underdetermined;
- an impersonal necessary foundation favored;
- a personal/agentive foundation favored;
- multiple live foundation families remaining;
- brute reality remaining adequate;
- framework-dependent underdetermination;
- insufficient signal.
