## 1. Acceptance of the independent audit

The independent auditor followed the frozen source order, did not modify repository state, did not repair the protocol, did not begin a theological study, and stopped after reporting the audit.

The audit identified two BLOCKING defects and a set of MAJOR defects that prevent Protocol 0.1.0 from qualifying for a second bounded stress test.

The program lead accepts the audit's central diagnosis:

> Protocol 0.1.0 is currently a strong checklist of methodological concerns, but it is not yet operational enough at the decisive transitions from evidence to disposition, from disposition to adjudication, and from adjudication to canonical acceptance.

## 2. Required findings accepted for repair

The following audit findings are accepted as valid qualification defects:

- **R1 — BLOCKING:** acceptance authority and audit independence;
- **R2 — BLOCKING:** no inferential bridge from evidence to adjudication to truth;
- **R3 — MAJOR:** miracle and revelation procedure;
- **R4 — MAJOR:** evidence burdens by claim type;
- **R5 — MAJOR:** claim-typing integrity;
- **R6 — MAJOR:** philosophical and non-historical evidence machinery;
- **R7 — MAJOR:** candidate-universe operationalization;
- **R8 — MAJOR:** discriminator discipline;
- **R9 — MAJOR:** source hierarchy;
- **R10 — MAJOR:** lane governance;
- **R11 — MAJOR:** evidence acquisition and link decomposition;
- **R12 — MAJOR:** origins/development/truth and continuity handling;
- **R13 — MAJOR:** Protocol–Governance–STATE alignment;
- **R14 — MAJOR:** audit mechanism details;
- **R15 — MAJOR:** uncertainty statement.

The MINOR findings R16 and R17 are also accepted and may be repaired in the same bounded repair because they concern the same protocol-operationalization surface and do not broaden theological scope.

## 3. Non-required improvements

The independent auditor's optional improvements are preserved but are **not qualification requirements**.

They may be adopted only where they directly simplify or clarify a required repair.

They must not expand the repair into a new research program or a second stress test.

## 4. Repair boundary

The repair is restricted to:

1. making the protocol operational and reproducible;
2. aligning it with Charter, Governance, STATE and Method Seed;
3. resolving the audit findings R1–R17;
4. freezing a focused independent re-audit gate.

The repair is **not authorized** to:

- begin the resurrection study;
- choose a theological candidate;
- modify EMT conclusions;
- reactivate ISR;
- create a program-level theological truth commitment;
- qualify Protocol 0.1.1 by self-attestation.

## 5. Qualification gate

Protocol 0.1.1, if produced, remains `CANDIDATE__REPAIR_PENDING_REAUDIT`.

Qualification requires a focused independent re-audit of R1–R15 using frozen criteria.

A re-audit result of `PASS` or `PASS_WITH_LIMITATIONS` permits the program lead to consider qualification.

Qualification remains separate from authorization of any second bounded stress test.
