# A1 — Exact truth question

TFP-STRESS-2 asks two nested questions.

## Q1 — Event proposition

> **Does the best publicly adjudicable historical evidence warrant the proposition that Jesus of Nazareth genuinely died by crucifixion and subsequently existed again in an embodied living state, such that a real postmortem embodied encounter process materially caused the earliest Christian resurrection proclamation and experience reports?**

Define:

`R_EVENT` = the proposition above.

"Embodied living state" is intentionally stronger than:
- a dream;
- bereavement vision;
- purely internal religious experience;
- literary symbol;
- memory construction;
- apparition with no embodied referent;
- survival from crucifixion without death.

It does **not** require the study to establish the later full doctrinal content of glorification, ascension, atonement, Trinity, scriptural inspiration, or any denomination's theology.

## Q2 — Causal/agent attribution

Only if Q1 receives sufficient warrant to make causal attribution meaningful:

> **What causal class, if any, is warranted for the event, and does the evidence warrant the further proposition that God raised Jesus from the dead?**

Q2 must preserve, as live where applicable:
- ordinary/known causal explanation;
- unknown cause;
- unspecified anomalous cause;
- unspecified supernatural cause;
- specific divine action.

A positive Q1 result does not mechanically imply a positive Q2 result.

A positive Q2 result does not mechanically establish Christianity as a whole.

# A2 — Scope

## Time period

Primary event window:
- the crucifixion period conventionally placed in the early first century CE;
- the emergence of the earliest resurrection proclamation and appearance traditions.

Primary source-evaluation window:
- first-century sources and source layers;
- early second-century material through approximately 150 CE where it can bear on transmission, dependence, reception, or development.

Later material may be used for:
- textual transmission;
- reception history;
- source dependence;
- preservation arguments;
- explicit later interpretation.

Later material may not be silently projected backward.

## Geography

Primary:
- Roman Judea and Galilee;
- Jerusalem;
- early eastern Mediterranean Christian networks materially relevant to the earliest surviving sources.

Other geography enters only when it bears on transmission, provenance, or source dependence.

## Languages

As relevant:
- Koine Greek;
- Aramaic/Hebrew substrate claims;
- Latin for Roman evidence.

The study does not require the program lead personally to possess specialist fluency. Specialist scholarship, critical editions, translations, and linguistic review must be provenance-recorded.

## Source universe

The frozen source plan must include, as relevant:
- earliest Christian textual sources;
- embedded earlier traditions/creedal or formulaic material where critically defensible;
- gospel/Acts traditions with explicit source-dependence analysis;
- early noncanonical Christian evidence inside the scope window;
- near-contemporary non-Christian evidence relevant to death, movement existence, or context;
- Roman crucifixion/execution evidence;
- Jewish burial/funerary evidence relevant to the case;
- textual-critical evidence for outcome-material passages;
- historical scholarship across confessional and nonconfessional positions;
- psychology/cognitive science relevant to bereavement experiences, visions, memory, group reports, contagion, and testimony;
- philosophy of testimony, historical inference, miracle/anomaly reasoning, and causal explanation;
- candidate-native/proponent scholarship;
- serious adverse/critical scholarship;
- methodological scholarship on ancient-history inference.

No source is elevated because it is canonical, extracanonical, skeptical, confessional, traditional, popular, or institutionally prestigious.

## Explicit exclusions

This study does **not** directly adjudicate:
- whether Christianity as a whole is true;
- the Trinity;
- incarnation as a complete doctrine;
- atonement theories;
- salvation/soteriology;
- biblical inspiration or inerrancy as global doctrines;
- papal, conciliar, denominational, or ecclesial authority;
- prophecy fulfillment unrelated to the specific event question;
- all miracles attributed to Jesus;
- all later Christian miracle claims;
- whether every gospel detail is historically true;
- whether every resurrection narrative detail is mutually harmonizable;

- the complete historicity/nonhistoricity question for Jesus as a separate program.

## Provisional exclusion requiring independent review

This exclusion is **not frozen as final** until the independent candidate-elicitation/exclusion reviewer determines whether excluding it would artificially narrow the candidate universe for Q1.

## Limits on downstream inference

- "Christianity is false";

# A6/B — Provisional candidate universe

This is the program lead's **initial** candidate set. It is intentionally not final until independent blind candidate elicitation occurs.

Each candidate is to be constructed in its strongest recognizable form. Caricatures are prohibited.

## C-R — EMBODIED_RESURRECTION_EVENT

Core claim:
- Jesus genuinely died;
- Jesus subsequently existed again as an embodied living person in a postmortem state;
- a real encounter process with that embodied Jesus materially caused the earliest resurrection appearance/proclamation stream.

Necessary propositions, initial:
- P-R1 actual death;
- P-R2 postmortem embodied life;
- P-R3 causal connection to foundational appearance/proclamation evidence.

This candidate does **not** by itself require the stronger claim that the specific causal agent has already been identified as God.

## C-S — SURVIVAL / APPARENT-DEATH

Core claim:
- Jesus did not actually die during/after crucifixion;
- later encounters with a surviving Jesus materially generated resurrection belief.

Necessary propositions, initial:
- P-S1 survival/non-death;
- P-S2 post-crucifixion encounters with the surviving Jesus;
- P-S3 survival plausibly accounts for the relevant proclamation/experience pattern.

## C-V — NON-BODILY EXPERIENTIAL / VISIONARY ORIGIN

Core claim:
- Jesus did not return to embodied postmortem life;
- one or more sincere non-bodily experiences/visions/apparitional experiences materially generated early appearance belief;
- social transmission and interpretation account for subsequent proclamation.

Necessary propositions, initial:
- P-V1 no embodied postmortem encounter process is required;
- P-V2 sufficiently early sincere experiential claims occurred;
- P-V3 known or defensible experiential/social mechanisms can account for the core evidence within scope.

This candidate does not assume that all witnesses were insincere or pathological.

## C-L — MEMORY / LEGEND / NARRATIVE DEVELOPMENT

Core claim:
- later robust bodily-resurrection narratives materially developed from earlier, thinner, ambiguous, symbolic, visionary, or otherwise non-equivalent traditions;
- memory, oral transmission, literary development, and community interpretation can account for the observed trajectory without an embodied resurrection event.

Necessary propositions, initial:
- P-L1 the earliest recoverable layer does not require the later robust embodied narrative;
- P-L2 the development sequence is chronologically and transmission-wise plausible;
- P-L3 the later evidence is sufficiently dependent/developmental to explain the stronger narrative without R_EVENT.

## C-F — DELIBERATE FABRICATION / STRATEGIC INVENTION

Core claim:
- one or more foundational resurrection claims were deliberately invented, strategically presented, or knowingly embellished by outcome-material early actors;
- later sincere belief may have developed from those claims.

Necessary propositions, initial:
- P-F1 deliberate invention at an outcome-material origin point;
- P-F2 a plausible motive/opportunity/transmission route;
- P-F3 resulting source/experience pattern can be explained without R_EVENT.

This candidate does not equate ordinary theological interpretation or memory error with fraud.

## C-BX — BODY-DISPOSITION ERROR/REMOVAL + NON-BODILY APPEARANCE PROCESS

Core claim:
- any tomb/body-disposition evidence relevant to resurrection is accounted for by burial uncertainty, relocation, removal, wrong-location identification, or another specified ordinary body-disposition mechanism;
- appearance/proclamation evidence is separately accounted for by a non-bodily experiential/developmental mechanism frozen before evidence adjudication.

Necessary propositions, initial:
- P-BX1 a specified body-disposition mechanism is historically viable;
- P-BX2 a specified non-bodily appearance/proclamation mechanism is historically viable;
- P-BX3 the two-factor model jointly explains the evidence without post-hoc tailoring.

## No free-form composite candidate in this cycle

A general "whatever mixture explains the evidence" candidate is **not** admitted.

If multiple supported components survive while no frozen candidate combines them adequately, use:
- `PROPOSED_REVISED_CANDIDATE_REQUIRED_WITHIN_SCOPE`.

A positive comparison of a newly built composite requires a fresh preregistered follow-up or genuinely held-out evidence, consistent with Protocol B5.

## Meta-outcomes remain outcomes, not candidates

- `NONE_ADEQUATE`;
- `UNDERDETERMINED`;
- `INSUFFICIENT_SIGNAL`.

Use `INSUFFICIENT_SIGNAL` only when **access, provenance, or coverage failure prevents the study from validly applying a required sufficiency rule, candidate-status rule, DIRECTION_RULE, or required background rerun at all** after documented effort.

Examples:
- an outcome-material source stratum required by the frozen plan cannot be accessed and no adequate substitute exists;
- provenance failure makes a CRITICAL evidence class uninterpretable;
- a CRITICAL comparison cannot reach MAKEABLE and remains outcome-relevant;
- background-sensitive inputs required for a mandatory rerun cannot be evaluated.

Do **not** use `INSUFFICIENT_SIGNAL` merely because:
- accessible evidence is weak;
- a claim is not established;
- adequately covered evidence is neutral;
