# TFP-STUDY-3-CONTINGENCY — B3 Steelman Packet: C-NEC-IMPERSONAL-CONCRETE 0.1.0

## Identity

- Candidate: `C-NEC-IMPERSONAL-CONCRETE`
- Frozen label: **Necessary impersonal concrete foundation**
- Status: **FROZEN_B3_PACKET__PENDING_EQUAL_STRENGTH_REVIEW**

## Candidate-native / primary formulation

At least one metaphysically necessary **concrete** but non-agentive reality, fact, substance, process, or immanent structure explains relevant contingent reality. The foundation is not an abstract principle and does not perform its explanatory work by intentional choice.

## Literature status

This is a literature-backed family but not a single named school. Contemporary cosmological literature explicitly recognizes **immanent necessity** as a serious alternative to transcendent necessary being. Spinoza provides a major historical model of necessary immanent reality, though his full “God or Nature” system is richer than the frozen TFP family.

## Strongest serious proponent / native sources

1. **Graham Oppy**, “O’Connor’s Cosmological Argument,” *Oxford Studies in Philosophy of Religion* 3 (2011): 166–186. Oppy identifies “immanent necessity” alongside infinite regress and brute contingency as a serious naturalistic option for ultimate explanation.
2. **Baruch Spinoza**, *Ethics*, Part I, as a historical worked model in which necessary substance/Nature is immanent rather than a contingent external object.
3. *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy*, “Spinoza’s Modal Metaphysics,” for the necessary-substance structure and the distinction between Spinoza's system and ordinary contingent beings.

Spinoza is an analogue, not a commitment to his theology, attributes, or necessitarianism in full.

## Core truth propositions

1. A metaphysically necessary concrete foundation exists.
2. It stands in the truth-relevant explanatory relation required for contingent reality.
3. The foundation is non-agentive in the explanatory role at issue.
4. Its necessity is not merely nomological necessity internal to a contingent setup.
5. The candidate does not require an abstract principle to do all of the ultimate explanatory work.

## Authority commitments

None. Historical use of “God” in Spinoza is not a religious-authority commitment and does not make this family theistic.

## Internal success conditions

- The foundation must be genuinely concrete under a defensible ontology.
- Its necessity must be independently motivated.
- It must explain, ground, constitute, or otherwise relate to contingent reality in a non-vacuous way.
- Non-agentive explanation must be sufficient for the relevant contingency.
- The position must avoid collapsing into C-NECESSITARIAN if it still affirms genuine contingent reality.

## Expected evidence / argumentative profile

The family expects any successful ultimate explanation to terminate immanently rather than in intentional agency. Its strongest support may be metaphysical or arise from an ontology on which the most fundamental concrete reality exists necessarily and derivative contingent structures depend on it. No particular physical theory is frozen into the candidate.

## Strongest recognized objections

1. **Necessity objection:** why think any concrete reality exists with metaphysical necessity?
2. **Modal-gap objection:** a necessary concrete base may fail to explain why contingent derivatives take one form rather than another.
3. **Agency challenge:** if the explanation must account for genuinely contingent alternatives, an impersonal necessary base may seem unable to select among them.
4. **Collapse objection:** if all consequences flow necessarily from the foundation, genuine contingency may disappear into C-NECESSITARIAN.
5. **Principle/concrete boundary objection:** the alleged concrete foundation may actually be functioning as a law, structure, or principle and belong under C-NEC-IMPERSONAL-PRINCIPLE.

## Common caricatures explicitly rejected

- The family is not automatically “God.”
- It is not automatically materialism.
- It does not assume one foundation rather than plural/layered variants.
- It does not require temporal causation.
- It does not identify metaphysical necessity with physical-law necessity.

## Packet contamination note

The packet uses Oppy’s immanent-necessity category as the closest contemporary family-level anchor and Spinoza as a historical analogue, while explicitly refusing to inherit their additional commitments without separate warrant.
