## Candidate-native / primary formulation

The ultimacy question is genuinely truth-apt, but reality itself does not determinately instantiate one of the rival determinate ultimacy structures. It is ontically indeterminate whether the relevant structure is brute, nonterminal, or necessary-grounded.

This is a claim about how reality is, not a report that investigators cannot decide among determinate alternatives.

## Literature status

The exact application to **ultimacy** is a synthetic TFP extension. The underlying metaphysical machinery—genuine metaphysical or ontic indeterminacy—is a substantial contemporary research position.

## Strongest serious proponent / native sources

1. **Elizabeth Barnes and J. Robert G. Williams**, “A Theory of Metaphysical Indeterminacy,” in *Oxford Studies in Metaphysics*, vol. 6 (2011), pp. 103–148. They develop a theory on which indeterminacy can be metaphysical rather than merely semantic or epistemic.
2. **Jessica M. Wilson**, “A Determinable-Based Account of Metaphysical Indeterminacy,” *Inquiry* 56.4 (2013): 359–385, DOI 10.1080/0020174X.2013.816251.
3. **J. Robert G. Williams**, “Ontic Vagueness and Metaphysical Indeterminacy,” *Philosophy Compass* 3.4 (2008): 763–788.

These sources defend general metaphysical-indeterminacy frameworks, not specifically an indeterminate cosmological ultimacy structure.

## Core truth propositions

1. The ultimacy question is truth-apt.
2. There is no unique determinate metaphysical answer at the relevant level.
3. The indeterminacy is worldly/ontic rather than linguistic or epistemic.
4. It is not equivalent to abstaining from judgment.
5. It is not Protocol B6 UNDERDETERMINED, which is an evidential/adjudicative state of the study.

## Authority commitments

None. The family depends on a metaphysics and logic of worldly indeterminacy.

## Internal success conditions

- A coherent account of metaphysical indeterminacy must apply at the ultimacy level.
- The account must distinguish worldly indeterminacy from vague language and limited evidence.
- The view must specify what is determinate enough for the claim of indeterminacy itself to be meaningful.
- It must explain why the candidate does not simply become a disjunction of ordinary determinate candidates.
- It must preserve classical/nonclassical logical commitments explicitly rather than exploiting ambiguity.

## Expected evidence / argumentative profile

The family is primarily metaphysical. Candidate-native support would come from an independently defensible theory of ontic indeterminacy and a reason to think ultimacy is among the domains to which that theory applies. It does not inherit support merely from the study's inability to discriminate candidates.

## Strongest recognized objections

1. **Epistemic-collapse objection:** alleged metaphysical indeterminacy may be ignorance in disguise.
2. **Semantic-collapse objection:** vagueness may belong to language or representation rather than reality.
3. **Determinacy-of-indeterminacy objection:** stating exactly where reality is indeterminate can appear to reintroduce determinacy at a higher level.
4. **Application objection:** even if metaphysical indeterminacy exists in some domains, it may not extend to ultimate explanatory structure.
5. **Disjunctive-surrogate objection:** the view may merely say one of several determinate candidates obtains, without there being a fact which one.

## Common caricatures explicitly rejected

- It is not “we cannot tell.”
- It is not agnosticism.
- It is not B6 UNDERDETERMINED.
- It does not say the question is meaningless.
- It does not require every feature of reality to be indeterminate.
